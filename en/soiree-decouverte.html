The Concept

To celebrate this immersive universe, D'LYR is organizing an exclusive Discovery Night on Saturday, October 3 from 8:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Reserved for the first 50 registered guests, this special event offers unlimited access to VR gaming experiences for the ultra-preferential rate of only €20! Participate as many times as you like.

Program for this exceptional evening:

• 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM: Zombie Shooter — Battle hordes of zombies as a team in an intense, collaborative action game.

• 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM: PVP (Player vs Player) — Go head-to-head against real players in high-octane competitive arena battles.

• 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM: Domination Mode — A strategic and dynamic game mode where two teams compete to capture and hold key control zones on the map.

⚠️ Exclusive capacity limited to 50 guests: to ensure maximum play time for everyone with minimal waiting time, entries are strictly limited. First come, first served!

Between arena games, join us for a drink in our lounge area. (Note: drinks and pizzas from the Bar are not included in the pass and remain available à la carte all evening).
