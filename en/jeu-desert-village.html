Desert Village

Book

Synopsis

Nestled among golden, sparkling dunes lies Desert Village, a peaceful little hamlet lined with palm trees and mud-brick houses. But this haven of peace is now in great danger! A ruthless, scheming developer has set his sights on the village: he wants to raze it all to build a gigantic oil complex.

To carry out his dark plan, he unleashes an army of little monsters… more cute than truly scary!

Armed with your courage and your team spirit, dive into the heart of the action as a family. In this bright cartoon world, join forces as a team to push back the waves of enemies, protect the villagers and preserve the magic of the village.

A magical, funny adventure that everyone can enjoy, perfect for taking your first steps into the wonderful world of virtual reality!
