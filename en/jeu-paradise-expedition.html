Paradise Expedition

Book

Synopsis

In the heart of an arid land forgotten by the world hides an unexpected sanctuary: a huge tropical greenhouse where nature has reclaimed its rights. Crystal-clear waterfalls, lush vegetation and vine-covered ruins form a setting as magnificent as it is dangerous.

But behind this heavenly oasis lies a merciless battleground. In this leafy labyrinth, every player fights for their own survival.

Here, no alliance is possible. Everyone plays for themselves. Only the fastest, the most accurate and the most strategic will manage to dominate this lost Eden.
