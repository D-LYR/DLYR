Brain Arena

Synopsis

Welcome to Brain Arena, the first immersive game show where your intelligence becomes your true superpower.

In a futuristic arena worthy of the greatest competition shows, you take on your friends in a series of general-knowledge challenges as fun as they are intense. History, cinema, music, sport, science, video games, geography or pop culture: pick your categories and get ready to fire up your neurons.

But here, knowing the answer isn't enough. You also have to be the fastest. Every second counts and every right answer can shake up the leaderboard.

Between fierce competition, bursts of laughter and surprises, Brain Arena turns general knowledge into a true immersive experience where anyone can become champion.
