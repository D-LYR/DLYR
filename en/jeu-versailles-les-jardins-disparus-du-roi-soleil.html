Synopsis

This experience allows visitors to explore iconic, now-vanished locations within the gardens, stepping straight into the 17th century for a poetic and interactive stroll guided by André Le Nôtre, gardener to Louis XIV.

A unique opportunity to discover the Royal Menagerie and its exotic animals, the Labyrinth Grove and its fountains, as well as the Grotto of Thetys with its exceptional rockwork decorations!
