🎃 Zombie Challenge

On October 30 and 31, from 8 PM to 11 PM, our VR arenas switch to zombie apocalypse mode for a team tournament. Headset on and weapon in hand, you move freely around the arena to fight off the zombie hordes with your teammates.

🎯 How it works: 5 teams of 8 players compete throughout the night, with 3 game sessions per person. Every zombie taken down earns points, and the scores from each session add up to push your team up the leaderboard. The team with the highest total score at the end of the night wins the tournament.

👥 Solo or with friends: no need to come as a group of 8, teams are formed on site as people arrive. Whether you come alone, as a pair or with a few friends, you'll join a team as soon as you arrive.

🏆 The prize: at the end of the tournament, each member of the winning team gets a free VR session of their choice for a future visit to D'LYR.

🍕 Between sessions: the bar and pizzas are available on site (at extra cost, not included in the price).

💰 Price: €25 per person, 3 VR sessions included. Only 40 spots each night: book ahead to make sure you get a place.
